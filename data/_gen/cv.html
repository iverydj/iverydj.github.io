Research Interests

I study how defects and disorder determine the electrical and optical properties of solid-state materials. I measure defect levels with transient spectroscopy (PICTS/DLTS) and map disorder and strain with Raman and tip-enhanced Raman nanospectroscopy (TERS). I also use interpretable machine learning to interpret experimental data physically, identifying which measured features reflect defects and disorder and how they connect to material properties.

Research

1. Defects and Dopants in Semiconductor Devices

  • Quantitative analysis of defect energy levels and densities in amorphous oxide semiconductor thin-film transistors by photo-induced current transient spectroscopy (PICTS), related to mobility and bias-stress stability. (Published: Surf. Interfaces 2025 (co-first author); Sci. Rep. 2023 (co-author); IEEE Trans. Electron Devices 2024 (co-author); ACS Appl. Electron. Mater. 2024 (co-author))
  • Converting the carrier type of two-dimensional Mo₁₋ₓWₓTe₂ through defect engineering to build complementary (n/p) inverters. (In preparation)
  • Extending PICTS to 2D semiconductors to resolve defect levels in atomically thin channels. (Ongoing)

2. Reading Defects and Disorder from Spectra

  • Predicting the electrical conductivity of carbon-nanotube films from Raman disorder bands, with explainable AI identifying which spectral features carry the information. (In preparation)
  • Denoising experimental Raman spectra of 2D materials with deep learning while preserving physical parameters such as peak position and width. (In preparation)
  • Review on when a machine-learning explanation of a spectrum counts as physical evidence. (In preparation)

3. Defects, Strain, and Excitons at the Nanoscale

  • Mapping local defects and strain in 2D materials with tip-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (TERS). (Ongoing)
  • Finite-difference time-domain (FDTD) modeling of gap-mode near-field enhancement to validate machine-learning predictions of TERS nanoprobe geometry. (Published: Mater. Des. 2026 (co-author))
  • Modulating excitons in 2D semiconductors through energy transfer and plasmon–exciton coupling in plasmonic nanostructures designed with GPU-accelerated FDTD. (Ongoing)
