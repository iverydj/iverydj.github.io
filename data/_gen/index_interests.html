My research asks how defects and disorder set the electrical and optical behavior of low-dimensional and oxide semiconductors. I measure them with electrical defect spectroscopy and Raman-family (nano)spectroscopy, and use interpretable machine learning to extract physical parameters from spectra and connect them to device behavior.
