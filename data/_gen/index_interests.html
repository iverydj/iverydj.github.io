I study how defects and disorder determine the electrical and optical properties of low-dimensional and oxide semiconductors. I measure defect levels with transient spectroscopy (PICTS/DLTS), map disorder and strain with Raman and tip-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (TERS), and use interpretable machine learning to extract physical parameters from spectra and relate them to electrical properties.
