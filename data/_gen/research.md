I study how defects and disorder determine the electrical and optical properties of low-dimensional and oxide semiconductors. I measure defect levels with <span class="rs-tech">transient spectroscopy (PICTS/DLTS)</span>, map disorder and strain with <span class="rs-tech">Raman and tip-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (TERS)</span>, and use <span class="rs-method">interpretable machine learning</span> to extract physical parameters from spectra and relate them to electrical properties.

### 1. Defects and Dopants in Semiconductor Devices

<p class="rs-question">How do defects and dopants control carrier type, trapping, and stability in semiconductor devices?</p>

- Quantitative analysis of defect energy levels and densities in <span class="rs-mat">amorphous oxide semiconductor</span> thin-film transistors by <span class="rs-tech">photo-induced current transient spectroscopy (PICTS)</span>, related to mobility and bias-stress stability. <span class="rs-status rs-status-published">Published</span> <span class="rs-refs"><a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.surfin.2025.106870">Surf. Interfaces 2025</a> (co-first author); <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-40162-0">Sci. Rep. 2023</a> (co-author); <a href="https://doi.org/10.1109/TED.2023.3347503">IEEE Trans. Electron Devices 2024</a> (co-author); <a href="https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaelm.4c01430">ACS Appl. Electron. Mater. 2024</a> (co-author)</span>
- Converting the carrier type of two-dimensional <span class="rs-mat">Mo₁₋ₓWₓTe₂</span> through defect engineering to build complementary (n/p) inverters. <span class="rs-status rs-status-in-preparation">In preparation</span>
- Extending PICTS to <span class="rs-mat">2D semiconductors</span> to resolve defect levels in atomically thin channels. <span class="rs-status rs-status-ongoing">Ongoing</span>

### 2. Reading Defects and Disorder from Spectra

<p class="rs-question">What can a spectrum tell us, quantitatively, about disorder and the properties it controls?</p>

- Predicting the electrical conductivity of <span class="rs-mat">carbon-nanotube</span> films from Raman disorder bands, with <span class="rs-method">explainable AI</span> identifying which spectral features carry the information. <span class="rs-status rs-status-in-preparation">In preparation</span>
- Denoising experimental Raman spectra of <span class="rs-mat">2D materials</span> with <span class="rs-method">deep learning</span> while preserving physical parameters such as peak position and width. <span class="rs-status rs-status-in-preparation">In preparation</span>
- Review on when a machine-learning explanation of a spectrum counts as physical evidence. <span class="rs-status rs-status-in-preparation">In preparation</span>
