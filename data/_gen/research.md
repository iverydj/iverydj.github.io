My research asks how defects and disorder set the electrical and optical behavior of low-dimensional and oxide semiconductors. I measure them with <span class="rs-tech">electrical defect spectroscopy</span> and <span class="rs-tech">Raman-family (nano)spectroscopy</span>, and use <span class="rs-method">interpretable machine learning</span> to extract physical parameters from spectra and connect them to device behavior.

### 1. Defects and Doping That Set Device Function

<p class="rs-question">How do defects and dopants control carrier type, trapping, and stability in semiconductor devices?</p>

- Quantified defect energy levels, densities, and trapping dynamics in <span class="rs-mat">amorphous oxide semiconductor</span> thin-film transistors with <span class="rs-tech">photo-induced current transient spectroscopy (PICTS)</span>, and related them to mobility and bias-stress stability. <span class="rs-status rs-status-published">Published</span>
- Controlling the carrier type of <span class="rs-mat">2D semiconductors</span> through defect engineering to build complementary (n/p) inverters. <span class="rs-status rs-status-in-preparation">In preparation</span>
- Extending PICTS to <span class="rs-mat">2D semiconductors</span> to resolve defect levels in atomically thin channels. <span class="rs-status rs-status-ongoing">Ongoing</span>

### 2. Reading Defects and Disorder from Spectra

<p class="rs-question">What can a spectrum tell us, quantitatively, about disorder and the properties it controls?</p>

- Predicting the electrical conductivity of <span class="rs-mat">carbon-nanotube</span> films from Raman disorder bands, with <span class="rs-method">explainable AI</span> identifying which spectral features carry the information. <span class="rs-status rs-status-in-preparation">In preparation</span>
- Recovering noisy, experimentally measured Raman spectra of <span class="rs-mat">2D materials</span> with <span class="rs-method">deep learning</span> while preserving physical parameters such as peak position and width. <span class="rs-status rs-status-in-preparation">In preparation</span>
- Writing an invited review on when an AI-derived explanation of a spectrum counts as physical evidence. <span class="rs-status rs-status-in-preparation">In preparation</span>

### 3. Defects, Strain, and Excitons at the Nanoscale

<p class="rs-question">What changes below the optical diffraction limit?</p>

- Mapping local defects and strain in <span class="rs-mat">2D materials</span> with <span class="rs-tech">tip-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (TERS)</span>. <span class="rs-status rs-status-ongoing">Ongoing</span>
- Modeled gap-mode near-field enhancement with <span class="rs-tech">finite-difference time-domain (FDTD)</span> simulations to validate machine-learning predictions of TERS nanoprobe geometry. <span class="rs-status rs-status-published">Published</span>
- Modulating excitons in <span class="rs-mat">2D semiconductors</span> through energy transfer and plasmon–exciton coupling in <span class="rs-mat">nanoparticle-on-mirror</span> cavities, designed with GPU-accelerated FDTD. <span class="rs-status rs-status-ongoing">Ongoing</span>
